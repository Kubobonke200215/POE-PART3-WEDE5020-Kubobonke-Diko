Our Services

Founded in 2024 by a group of local activists and retired legal practitioners, the Mount Frere Siyakhana legal advice centre was built to address the critical gap in legal aid and social services across the rural in the Eastern Cape, operating in the Alfred Nzo District, the centre began as a small drop-in consultation desk assisting elderly community members with pension and SASSA grant applications.
